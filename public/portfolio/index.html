Case 01, startup founder, United States, June to October 2026

See what a change breaks before you make it

A web app that maps how the parts of a product depend on each other, so the team sees the impact of a change before anyone touches it. Delivered over four stages, each approved by the client.

16xmore data, and the trace view kept its speed

What they needed

Before changing a part, the team needed to see everything that change reaches: other parts, requirements, software and drawings. Their data arrives as spreadsheet exports, each team in its own format.

What I built

  • A graph engine that follows seven rules for how a change spreads, each with a set direction, showing the path behind every result.
  • Import that reads each team's own spreadsheet format and keeps every column.
  • Suggested links for parts left unconnected, which people confirm or reject, with undo and a record of who confirmed each link and when.
  • A data browser with search, consistent editing, depth and type filters, and sign in for the whole team.

What came of it

Four stages, each approved. The stage 3 acceptance test set a five second target for a result on 1,000 records, and the client accepted it. On record: the stage agreements with their acceptance tests, evidence documents, and before and after screen recordings. The code lives in the client's own GitHub.

Case 03, software company, United States, January to February 2026

A product roadmap moved to Planner Basic

A roadmap moved from Planner Premium to a simpler plan type by a flow, with no copying by hand.

400 plusroadmap tasks moved

What they needed

Planner Basic has no task hierarchy and different fields, and the move had to run inside their own Microsoft 365 tenant.

What I built

  • A Power Automate flow that reads the roadmap, keeps only the sprints that matter and creates the tasks.
  • Parent tasks become cards and child tasks become checklist items, ticked when done.
  • Labels and buckets mapped by rule, and each task's Teams links carried into its notes.
  • Built inside their own tenant as a portable package, with the group and plan as inputs, so it can run again on another plan.

What came of it

More than 400 tasks moved with the structure kept. On record: the delivery checklist and the exported flow.

Case 06, wellness business, United Kingdom, July to September 2026

Sign ups that flow straight into email

Assessment results wired into the CRM, and the email domain moved and secured.

23 of 23DNS records verified after the move

What they needed

Each assessment result had to reach their CRM, and their domain had to move to Cloudflare with its email records in order.

What I built

  • A Cloudflare Worker that joins Typeform to Systeme.io and tags each person with their result.
  • Browser links that carry a lead id the Worker issues, never an email address.
  • Email address checks with Kickbox, and alerts sent from a subdomain of their own.
  • Separate staging and production with a parity check that compares them, and GDPR processor documentation.

What came of it

Four milestones accepted, with SPF, DKIM, DMARC and DNSSEC all in place on the new DNS. On record: the test matrix, the evidence packs, the handover note and the parity check.

  • Workers KV

Case 07, holiday accommodation, United States, January to February 2026

Bookings sent from WordPress to Beds24

Bookings taken on their own website are sent to their channel manager, for six units.

6units mapped to Beds24

What they needed

Each booking taken on their own site had to reach Beds24, and changes and cancellations had to follow.

What I built

  • A WordPress plugin that sends each confirmed booking to Beds24 through a background queue, so the payment step is not held up.
  • Cancellations free the dates again, and date changes move the booking.
  • Rates sync every night, and dates blocked by hand sync too.
  • A queue that runs five jobs a minute, a resync panel and a log in the admin.

What came of it

Zero double bookings across the six units. On record: the plugin source and the go live checklist.

  • PHP
  • HBook
  • Beds24 API
  • WP Cron
